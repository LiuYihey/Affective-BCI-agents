# How do I wire a decoder into a loop that stays safe?

Sense turns the signal into a belief, Think proposes an action, Act decides what may reach the user. A harness keeps an adaptive system inside the range that was tested.

**28 references** list here; 12 are filed primarily under this question.

## What to look for

- Sense / Think / Act
- Action gate
- Agent harness
- Contingency and control groups
