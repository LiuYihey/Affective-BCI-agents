# Which corpus should I use?

Where the signal comes from, how the label was produced, and how many people it covers. Labels assigned by the experimenter, reported by the participant, or read from behaviour are not interchangeable.

**28 references** list here; 18 are filed primarily under this question.

## What to look for

- Signal source and quality
- Target definition
- Corpus size
- Annotation cost
