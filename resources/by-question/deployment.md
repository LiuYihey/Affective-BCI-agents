# Can I run this in real time, on real hardware?

A fast classifier can still respond too late. Output reaches the user through a display, an interface, or a stimulation device, and each destination has its own risk and its own approver.

**26 references** list here; 12 are filed primarily under this question.

## What to look for

- Latency and energy
- Monitoring and assessment
- Neurofeedback
- Responsive neuromodulation
