# Where is the boundary of what the system may do?

A neural recording can reveal more than the purpose for which it was collected. Consent, authorisation and deletion need their own rules.

**7 references** list here; 3 are filed primarily under this question.

## What to look for

- Re-identification risk
- Consent scope
- Retained state
- Clinician oversight
