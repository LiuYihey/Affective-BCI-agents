# How strong is the evidence, and how mature is the system?

Lab asks whether the state can be estimated. Pilot lets the estimate change something during a limited experiment. Field uses the complete system repeatedly in its intended setting.

**19 references** list here; 16 are filed primarily under this question.

## What to look for

- Lab / Pilot / Field
- Reported gaps in pilot studies
- Reproducibility
- Outcome measures
