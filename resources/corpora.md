# Corpora

Every corpus in the field, grouped by how the target was obtained.
The grouping matters more than the size: a label assigned by the
experimenter, reported by the participant, or read from behaviour
answer different questions, and a number from one group does not
transfer to another.

## Stimulus-elicited targets

| Corpus | Scale | Modality | Target | How the target is obtained | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SEED** | 15 participants; 62 channels; 3 sessions | Scalp EEG | Three emotion classes | Experimenter-assigned film-clip condition, copied to every window of the trial | [source](https://doi.org/10.1109/tamd.2015.2431497) |
| **SEED-IV** | 15 participants; 62 channels; 3 sessions | Scalp EEG | Four emotion classes | Experimenter-assigned film-clip condition | [source](https://doi.org/10.1109/tcyb.2018.2797176) |
| **SEED-V** | 16 participants; 3 sessions | Scalp EEG plus eye tracking | Five emotion classes | Experimenter-assigned film-clip condition | [source](https://doi.org/10.1109/tcds.2021.3071170) |
| **FACED** | 123 participants; 32 channels | Scalp EEG | Nine emotion classes | Experimenter-assigned video condition | [source](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02650-w) |

| Corpus | Scale | Modality | Target | How the target is obtained | Source |

| **DEAP** | 32 participants; 32 channels | Scalp EEG | Continuous valence and arousal | Post-trial self-rating on fixed valence-arousal scales | [source](https://doi.org/10.1109/t-affc.2011.15) |
